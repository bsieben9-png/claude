How to use this app

This app shows what's on the Country Cannabis menu in Calmar right now, what changed, and which products give you the most for your money. The menu is checked three times a day.

Browsing the menu

  • The Menu tab lists everything in stock. Tap any product to open it: you'll see the description, a bigger picture, cannabinoid amounts, price per gram or per mg, and its price history.
  • Use the All categories list to show one category, like Vapes, Flower, Pre-rolls or Edibles.
  • New this week shows only products that arrived in the last 7 days. Include gone also shows products that sold out or left the menu recently.
  • Show more at the bottom loads the next batch of products.

Filters for each category

  • After you pick a category, a row of extra filters appears that fits that category. Vapes can be filtered by type, hardware, extract and flavour; flower by size and strain; pre-rolls by pack size, and so on.
  • With All categories selected, the Cannabinoid filters let you narrow the list to THC-dominant, Balanced or CBD-dominant products, or to ones with CBN or CBG.
  • Each choice shows how many products it matches. Tap Clear to reset them.

Sorting

  • Best value (the default) puts the cheapest THC per mg first. Smokables and vapes show the price per 100 mg of THC; edibles and drinks show the price per 10 mg.
  • Cheapest CBD and Cheapest CBN do the same for CBD or CBN, handy for sleep or wellness products.
  • Products the store doesn't list THC for go to the end of the value sorts. Other sorts include newest, most THC, your rating, price and name.

Searching

  • Type a few words in any order, like blue dream preroll or spinach vape. Every word has to match somewhere in the name, brand, SKU, category or strain type.
  • Search always looks through every category at once, so you see all the vapes, pre-rolls, flower and edibles that fit. To narrow it down, pick a category after you type.
  • Common ways of saying the same thing all work: pre-roll, pre roll, preroll and joint; cart, cartridge and 510; gummy, gummies and soft chew; disty and distillate.
  • Small typos are forgiven, so gumy still finds gummies.

Your ratings and notes

  • Open a product and tap the stars (1 to 5) to rate it, and write notes on taste, effects or hardware. Everything saves on its own.
  • Ratings and notes are private: each person who uses the app has their own, and nobody else sees them.
  • Use the Rated by me list to see what you've rated, including things that aren't on the menu right now. If a rated product comes back, your rating shows up on it again.

Watchlist and alerts

  • Tap the bell next to any product to watch it. The Watchlist tab shows its price and any changes: price drops, sales, restocks, or leaving the menu.
  • Set Alert at or below $ to get flagged when it hits your price. A banner at the top shows watchlist news from the last 3 days.
  • Your watchlist is private too.

Check now and the change log

  • This Chrome version checks the store from your computer at 8:52 AM, 4:20 PM and midnight while Chrome is open. If Chrome was closed, a missed check runs when it starts. The time of the last check is at the top.
  • Check now runs an extra check right away. Results show up by themselves in about a minute, and a Chrome notification lists anything new.
  • The Change log link in the footer lists every check: new products, price and sale changes, and what left the menu.

Product photos

  • Some products only have the store's plain placeholder picture. For those, the app looks for the real product photo in the Ontario Cannabis Store's public catalog.
  • A photo it's confident about is shown with a Matched photo badge when you open the product. It's the same product but might be a slightly different pack size.
  • If the store's own picture is just a generic stock box, the owner can tap Store photo is just a stock image? and the next check looks for the real photo.
  • When it isn't sure, the owner sees a Pick photo button with up to three likely photos to choose from, plus No photo. The owner can also change or undo any matched photo.

Only in-stock items are listed, so "gone" means sold out or delisted. Prices and potency come from the store's online menu.
